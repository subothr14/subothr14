## What I do

I founded **SetaByte**, a software studio building web, mobile and AI products, and I'm Team Lead and Director of IT Development at **BBDSP Consultant**, where I lead a team of 16+ developers.

I've been shipping production software since 2020, starting as a freelancer at 18. My work spans B2B and B2C platforms, crypto wallets, AI desktop tools and point-of-sale systems, for clients in government, hospitality, healthcare, agriculture and retail.

Right now I'm finishing a BIT at the University of Moratuwa and working through Microsoft's AI Engineer learning path.
